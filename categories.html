Advanced Mode: CSV

Download, edit, and upload your entire category configuration as CSV

CSV Format Guide (3 Columns)

Header: action,source_category,target_category

MAPPING: Plaid category to user label
Example: MAPPING,BANK_FEES_ATM_FEES,Bank Fees: Atm Fees

SET_CUSTOM: Custom category (leave source_category empty)
Example: SET_CUSTOM,,Pet Expenses: Dog Food

RETARGET: Move everything on the old label (transactions, rules, overrides, bills and their split templates, spending alerts) to a new label in your uploaded list
Example: RETARGET,Old: Label,New: Label

KEEP: Keep a label you dropped from the mappings as a custom category with no Plaid category. Each of its Plaid transactions without an override gets one, so it keeps the label instead of following its Plaid category. Rules, bills and alerts on it are untouched.
Example: KEEP,Old: Label

RETARGET_KEY: Move a Plaid category's past transactions to its new label
Example: RETARGET_KEY,FOOD_AND_DRINK_COFFEE

KEEP_KEY: Keep a Plaid category's past transactions on the old label; each gets an override
Example: KEEP_KEY,FOOD_AND_DRINK_COFFEE

EXISTING_RULES_RETARGET / EXISTING_OVERRIDES_RETARGET: Older forms that move only rules, or only overrides; still accepted
Example: EXISTING_RULES_RETARGET,Old: Label,New: Label

Format: All target values must follow format Primary: Detailed. Lines starting with # are ignored.

When a label leaves your list: if a label you drop is still used (the download's # In use: block lists the counts), the upload needs to know where its records go. If every Plaid category that pointed at it now points at one label that wasn't in your list before (a rename), the upload moves everything for you. Otherwise, including a move into a label you already have (a merge), the upload is refused and the error gives the exact RETARGET and KEEP lines to add. Add one and upload again. Remapping a Plaid category whose past transactions carry the old label also needs a RETARGET_KEY or KEEP_KEY line. Choosing a file shows a preview of what the upload would do; nothing changes until you press Apply.
